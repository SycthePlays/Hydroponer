# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Hydroponer to be a project where anyone — a first-time grower, an experienced horticulturist, a developer, someone whose first language is not English — can contribute and be treated well.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds the project:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people who will use it
- Showing empathy toward other contributors
- Assuming good faith, especially across a language barrier

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing someone's contribution on the basis of who they are rather than what they wrote

## Disagreement is fine

This project's documentation is written to be argued with, and its architecture decision records exist to be contested. Disagreeing strongly with an idea is welcome and useful. Disagreeing with a *person* is not the same thing, and that is the line.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any public space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers through a private channel. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed without their consent.

Maintainers will:

1. Review the report promptly and fairly
2. Respond in a way proportionate to what happened — a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban
3. Explain the decision to the person reported, where doing so is safe

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided by the project's other maintainers.
